Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the weather forecast?

Our forecasts are powered by the Indian Meteorological Department and other trusted global weather sources. Short-term forecasts (1–3 days) have up to 90% accuracy.

How often is the data updated?

Weather data is updated every hour to ensure the latest conditions are reflected, including temperature, wind, humidity, and alerts.

Can I see weather for multiple cities?

Yes! Just type the name of the city in the search box on the homepage or forecast page. You’ll instantly get current and upcoming weather updates.

Why is my location not showing correctly?

This might happen if location permissions are disabled on your browser or device. Enable location access or manually enter your city name.

What units are used for temperature and wind?

We use Celsius (°C) for temperature and kilometers per hour (km/h) for wind speed. In future updates, users will be able to switch units.

Is there a mobile app available?

Currently, we are working on an Android and iOS app for WeatherSphere. Stay tuned to our homepage for announcements and updates!
