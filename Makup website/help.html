Help & FAQ

1. How do I place an order?
To place an order, simply browse through our categories and products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You will be prompted to enter your payment and shipping details.
2. Can I cancel my order after placing it?
Once an order has been placed, it is processed for shipping immediately. Unfortunately, we are unable to cancel orders once they are confirmed. However, please contact customer support as soon as possible, and we may be able to assist you.
3. How do I track my order?
Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email with tracking details. You can use these details to track your order on the courier's website.
4. How do I return an item?
We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. To return an item, please contact our customer service team to receive a return authorization number. For more details, visit our Returns & Exchanges page.
5. What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and other secure online payment methods.
6. Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we offer international shipping. Please check our shipping policy for specific countries we ship to and the rates applicable.
7. Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
If your order has not yet been processed, please contact customer support immediately. Unfortunately, we are unable to change the shipping address once the order has been dispatched.
8. How do I use a discount code?
You can enter your discount code during the checkout process. The discount will be applied automatically to your order total.
9. How do I reset my account password?
If you've forgotten your password, simply click on the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page. You will be sent an email with instructions to reset your password.
10. Can I change my order once it has been placed?
Once an order is placed, we cannot make changes to it. Please make sure your order details are correct before confirming the purchase.
